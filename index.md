## Projects

### 1. NBA Claim Lab — Did High-Scoring Players Become More Common?

**Status:** Completed

**Research question:** Among eligible NBA player-seasons (games ≥ 40, minutes ≥ 15),
did the share averaging 20+ points per game rise across the ten regular seasons
from 2015-16 to 2024-25?

**Findings:** The share roughly doubled, from **7.04% in 2015-16** to
**14.92% in 2024-25**, peaking at **18.12% in 2022-23**. The trend is robust to
relaxing the games-played cutoff from 40 to 20, though it is not perfectly linear.
